UAE / MENA AI Policy Search

Shipped

A CLI semantic search tool over six original summary documents covering UAE and Saudi AI policy — National AI Strategy 2031, PDPL, SDAIA/NSDAI, and cross-jurisdiction comparisons. Built on a transparent TF-IDF + cosine similarity pipeline rather than a black-box embedding model, by deliberate choice — fully explainable, zero model download, sub-second indexing. Tested with 7 automated checks covering ranking correctness.

Information retrieval TF-IDF Python

Berth Scheduling: Greedy vs. Exact MILP

Shipped

A port berth-scheduling problem solved two ways and measured, not assumed — a list-scheduling heuristic against an exact MILP formulation (PuLP/CBC). On small instances, exact solving beats the heuristic by ~38%; on larger, congested ones, the MILP's combinatorial growth means it can't even match the heuristic in time. A small cousin of the PPO/GAE port-scheduling work in my main portfolio.
